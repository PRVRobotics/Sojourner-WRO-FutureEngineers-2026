# Competition Videos
In this section we can find the videos related to our robot compleating the several parts of the competition challenges.

## Open Challenge

In the Open Challenge, our robot must autonomously complete three laps around the track as quickly and accurately as possible. The placement of the inside track walls can change randomly between rounds, so our robot must navigate the course while adapting to the different track configurations and staying within the boundaries.

## Obstacle Challenge

In the Obstacle Challenge, our robot must autonomously complete three laps while navigating randomly placed red and green traffic signs. Our robot must pass red pillars on the right and green pillars on the left without moving them, and after completing the three laps, it must locate the parking area and perform a parallel-parking maneuver.
